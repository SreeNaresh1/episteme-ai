1. Executive Summary

Over 80% of Indian college graduates in engineering and technology face an employability gap due to a lack of industry-aligned, practical project competencies. While elite universities worldwide offer free public courseware, self-directed learners are blocked by three structural hurdles: surprise paywalls (commercial platforms masquerading as free, only to demand subscriptions midway), link rot (over 30% of web syllabi break into 404 errors within 18 months), and unbudgeted cognitive overload (generic chatbots producing unrealistic 40-hour weekly plans, causing a 90%+ dropout rate).

Episteme AI is an autonomous, agentic curriculum engineering system that transforms ambiguous career goals into strictly constrained, prerequisite-ordered weekly roadmaps. Powered exclusively by 100% verified free public courseware (Coursera Free Audit, Kaggle Learn, freeCodeCamp, MIT OCW), the system combines a mathematical capacity solver (e.g., 5h/week for 26 weeks = 130h exact budget), an anti-scraping HTTP crawler with headless DOM paywall inspection, an autonomous open-web self-healing engine that hot-swaps broken or paid links, 1-click Google Calendar / RFC-5545 scheduling, and cryptographically verified digital graduation certificates.

2. Problem Statement & Socio-Economic Need in India

For India to realize the vision of Viksit Bharat @ 2047, high-tier upskilling must be democratized for students across Tier-2, Tier-3, and rural institutions. Today's educational landscape presents critical failure modes:

  • The Commercial Ed-Tech Trap: Upskilling bootcamps charge between ₹50,000 and ₹2,50,000, creating an insurmountable economic barrier for middle-class and rural Indian families.
  • Curriculum Decay & Link Rot: Free learning roadmaps found on YouTube or GitHub rot rapidly without automated verification. Students hit broken links and decommissioned coding exercises with zero fallback.
  • Lack of Structured Capacity Modeling: College students balancing coursework and exams have limited weekly study hours (typically 5 to 10 hours). Existing AI chatbots hallucinate bloated plans without time-budget constraints, leading to immediate abandonment.
  • Absence of Verifiable Outcomes: Self-taught learners lack proof of completion, making employers skeptical of their practical competencies.
The "Seva First" Imperative
Episteme AI provides Zero-Tuition Educational Equity. By automating the discovery and audit of legitimate free university education, it saves underprivileged students ₹50,000–₹1,50,000 in tuition while providing institutional-grade learning rigor.

3. System Architecture & Multi-Agent Workflow

Episteme AI operates as a deterministic, stateful multi-agent state machine comprising six synchronized stages:

Figure 1: Complete 6-Stage Multi-Agent Architecture & Stateful Persistence Tier
  1. Capacity Intake & Integer Solver: Ingests learner ambition (e.g., Data Analyst in 6 months @ 5h/week). Formulates exact integer budget feasibility ($26\text{ weeks} \times 5\text{h/week} = 130\text{ hours}$ exact). Enforces an absolute weekly cap of $\le 5$ hours.
  2. Prerequisite DAG Reasoning Engine: Leverages Google Gemini 2.0 Flash and topological sorting to construct a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) spanning Foundations $\to$ Relational SQL $\to$ Python Analysis $\to$ Capstone Defense, guaranteeing zero duplicate topics.
  3. Anti-Scraping Crawler & Headless DOM Inspector: Employs Chrome 124 Sec-Ch-Ua headers and Cheerio to parse HTML DOM and Schema.org isAccessibleForFree JSON-LD, classifying true free resources vs. paid traps with 1,200ms timeout SLA protection.
  4. Autonomous Open-Web Healing: Upon encountering dead links or paywalls, formulates dynamic search queries targeting open courseware (site:edu OR site:org), inspects candidates in real time, and hot-swaps verified free substitutes into SQLite.
  5. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gate: Prevents unauthorized actions. The student inspects the roadmap and approves external dispatch with one click.
  6. Live Platform Enrollment & Calendar Sync: Dispatches real Coursera Free Audit deep links (courseAudit=true), Kaggle tokens, and builds 1-Click Google Calendar event sync URLs alongside universal RFC-5545 .ics files with unique idempotency keys.
  7. Long-Term Outcomes Tracking & Certification: Tracks 26 weekly practical project deliverables and issues a cryptographically signed SHA-256 graduation certificate upon 100% completion.

6. Cost Analysis, Scalability & Implementation Roadmap

Cost Structure: Operating costs are exceptionally low. Serving a student for a full 26-week curriculum costs less than ₹1.50 per learner per year, utilizing lightweight edge serverless functions and efficient Gemini 2.0 Flash token usage (~4,000 tokens / 8 paise per curriculum generation).

Implementation Plan:

  • Phase 1 (Months 1–3): Campus pilot across 300 engineering students in our institution targeting Data Analytics and Software Development campus placements.
  • Phase 2 (Months 4–6): Consortium rollout across affiliated colleges and student technical clubs (GDG, ACM, IEEE chapters).
  • Phase 3 (Months 7–12): Integration with national open-learning repositories (NPTEL, SWAYAM, AICTE Free Resources) for nationwide automated scheduling.
